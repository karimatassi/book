FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is this for?

Executives and high performers with a clear goal: more energy at work, weight loss, a marathon, better sleep or beating burnout.

Is it worth $699?

Most executives see providers who never talk to each other, and still guess at what they're missing. Here, one MD assesses all 7 areas, writes your report, builds your 90-day plan and matches you to the right providers. Covered by the guarantee.

I'm busy. How much time does it take?

A 15-minute form and one 90-minute video call. Then discovery calls with the providers you choose. Sessions run weekdays 12–3 pm and 6–9 pm, and Saturdays 10 am–12 pm.

What is the 90-minute session like?

A structured video conversation with Karim, not therapy. He asks about each area of your life and how it affects your goal. You choose what to share.

I already have a coach, a doctor or a therapist. What does this add?

Each of them looks at one area. Karim looks at all seven, shows which one is driving the rest, and finds the gaps in your team. Keep them all: they can be part of your care team.

*What does "Results or 100% money back" mean?

Attend your assessment and work through your plan. If you still don't feel it helped you make a transformational change within 90 days, get a full refund, no questions asked.

I don't have one clear goal. Is that okay?

Yes. Then finding it is the goal. You leave with two directions to test and a date to choose one. It's not a personality test: we look at what's actually happening in your life. See a sample plan →

Do I pay the providers separately?

Yes. Your $699 covers the assessment, your report, your 90-day plan and your personalized wellness care team referrals. You choose which providers to see, and each bills you directly.
