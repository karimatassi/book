FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is this for?

Mostly corporate professionals aged 25 to 35 who are successful on paper, but whose social life is affecting their health: vices costing them their self-esteem, work anxiety, a feed that leaves them anxious, loneliness, or dating that wears them down.

Is it worth $799?

Separate providers for each area easily cost $3,000+, and none of them connect the pieces. Here, one MD assesses all 7 areas, writes your report, builds your 90-day plan and reviews it with you. Covered by the guarantee.

I'm busy. How much time does it take?

A 15-minute form and two video calls: 90 minutes, then 30. Sessions run weekdays 12–3 pm and 6–9 pm, and Saturdays 10 am–12 pm. Everything is by video.

What is the 90-minute session like?

A structured video conversation with Karim, not therapy. He asks about each area of your life and how it affects your goal. You choose what to share.

I already have a coach, a doctor or a therapist. What does this add?

Each of them looks at one area. The program looks at all seven and shows which one is driving the rest. Keep them all, and bring them your report.

*What does "Results or 100% money back" mean?

Attend both sessions and work through your plan. If you still don't feel it helped you make a transformational change within 90 days, get a full refund, no questions asked. Book only if you plan to show up and do the work.

I don't have one clear goal. Is that okay?

Yes. Then finding it is the goal. You leave with two directions to test and a date to choose one. It's not a personality test: we look at what's actually happening in your life. See a sample plan →

What happens after my review?

You keep your report, your plan and the AI-ready copy, and work the plan on your own. The program stands on its own. GROW and TRANSFORM are optional.
