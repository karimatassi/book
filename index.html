FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is this for?

Corporate professionals, usually in their 30s, with one goal they've been putting off: starting a business, a promotion, getting back in shape, a relationship, or getting out of burnout.

Is it worth $997?

Separate coaches for your career, your body and your relationships easily cost $3,000+, and none of them connects the pieces. Here, one MD looks at all 7 areas, builds your plan, coaches you for 30 days and books your specialists. Covered by the double guarantee.

I'm busy. How much time does it take?

One 90-minute video session, then three 30-minute calls over 30 days, plus the weekly actions in your plan. Sessions run weekdays 12–3 pm and 6–9 pm, and Saturdays 10 am–12 pm.

What is the 90-minute session like?

A structured video conversation with Karim, not therapy. He asks about each area of your life and how it affects your goal. You choose what to share.

What happens after 30 days?

You keep your Blueprint and your before-and-after Life Map. If you showed up to every call, did your weekly actions and still missed your target, Karim keeps coaching you for free until you hit it.

I've tried coaches, apps and plans before. Why would this work?

Most plans ask you for more willpower. The Sprint changes what's around your goal instead: your evenings, your circle, your calendar. And if you do the work and still miss your day-30 target, Karim keeps coaching you for free until you hit it.

I already have a coach, a doctor or a therapist. What does this add?

Each of them looks at one area. Karim looks at all seven, shows which one is driving the rest, and finds the gaps. Keep them all: they become part of your plan.

*How does the double guarantee work?

1. If your first session doesn't give you a plan you believe in, email Karim and you get a full refund. 2. If you show up to every call, do your weekly actions and miss your day-30 target, Karim keeps coaching you for free until you hit it.

I don't have one clear goal. Is that okay?

Yes. Then finding it is the goal. You leave the first session with two directions to test and a date to choose one. See a sample plan →

Do I pay the specialists separately?

Yes. Your $997 covers the diagnosis, your Blueprint, the Sprint Calls, the scripts and booking your specialists. Each specialist you choose bills you directly.

Who are the specialists?

Vetted, licensed providers in Toronto, matched to what your plan needs. You choose who you see.

Why only 8 clients a month?

Every Blueprint is written by hand within 48 hours, and every client gets weekly calls with Karim. Eight a month is how many he can do well.

Can I try something free first?

Yes. Take the free MD-led Life Assessment: 28 questions, your 7-area map, and how to fix your 2 weakest areas.
