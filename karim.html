Part 1 · The beginning

He became a doctor.

In medical school, Karim realized that most of his patients' problems came from blind spots: areas of their health that weren't physical or mental at all.

Their careers, their money, their relationships and the places they lived were quietly shaping how they felt.

Part 2 · The turning point

He was living it too.

During medical school, Karim was also:

  • Scaling a startup that raised $1.6M
  • Closing $2M+ in B2B sales
  • Building some of the largest award-winning student-run communities
  • Serving as a G20 Young Entrepreneurs' Alliance delegate and a World Economic Forum Global Shaper

He was burning out. He started taking Vyvanse for ADHD, omega-3s and every supplement he could find to boost his performance.

Nothing really worked.

Part 3 · Where it led

So he built a framework that changed everything.

After residency, Karim developed the framework that transformed his own life: the 7 Dimensions. Instead of pushing harder on willpower, he tuned every area of his health and life toward one goal.

Today he teaches it to executives and helps them use it to boost their performance and reach their goals.

Today

Now he builds it with you.

Karim teaches the 7 Dimensions in workshops and one-on-one sessions. It has helped 100+ people.
