Part 1 · The beginning

I trained as a doctor.

In clinic, I noticed that most of what my patients came in with wasn't biological. It came from the environments they lived in: where they lived, the career they were in, how much money they had, and the relationships closest to them.

And it showed up as physical symptoms.

Part 2 · The turning point

I was living it too.

On paper, I was winning:

  • Scaled a startup and raised $1.6M
  • Closed $2M+ in B2B sales
  • Built some of the largest award-winning student-run communities
  • Delegate for the G20 Young Entrepreneurs' Alliance and a World Economic Forum Global Shaper

All while managing medical school, on willpower alone. And I felt unwell.

Then, in residency, I burnt out.

Part 3 · Where it led

So I built a 7-area model, and used it on myself.

Instead of treating each symptom on its own, I started mapping my patients' lives across all seven areas and helping them change their environment.

Many came back with remarkably positive results.

When I burnt out, I applied it to myself: I stopped relying on willpower and optimized my environment, until all seven areas were pulling in the same direction.

Today

Now I build it with you.

I share this model in workshops and one-on-one sessions. It has helped 100+ people.
