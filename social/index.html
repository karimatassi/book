Questions, answered.

Who is this for?

Ambitious professionals who want a partner, real friends, mentors, a circle that matches where they're going, or the confidence to meet them.

Why an MD?

Your relationships and surroundings are part of your health. Karim earned his MD at McGill and completed family medicine at McMaster. He's trained to find the one cause behind many symptoms.

How much time does it take?

Assessment: a 90-minute video session and 3 check-ups of 20 minutes. Core and Platinum add a 30-minute session every week for 30 days. Sessions run weekdays 12–3 pm and 6–9 pm, and Saturdays 10 am–12 pm.

Is this dating or networking coaching?

It covers both, and more. The goal is a healthier social life: the right people, the right places, and the confidence to keep them.

What are the bonuses?

Your City Map: the places you should be going. Your Event system: the events worth your time, sent to you every week. Your LinkedIn system: builds your list and drafts your messages; you click send, nothing is sent automatically. Core includes 1, Platinum all 3.

How does the guarantee work?

Core and Platinum: retake your assessment at day 30. If your score hasn't improved and you did everything, we keep coaching you free until it does, or you can choose a full refund. "Did everything" means you attended every session and completed all homework and surveys.

Can I try something free first?

Yes. Take the free Social Health Assessment and see your map in a few minutes.

Is what I share private?

Yes. Only Karim sees it. It's used only for your plan, never shared, and deleted when you ask.

Is this healthcare or therapy?

No. It's coaching. Karim doesn't diagnose, treat or prescribe here. When you need clinical care, you'll be referred to a licensed professional.
