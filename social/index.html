Our approach

We look at your whole life, then tune it to your goal.

At SE7EN, we review your whole life to understand how your social life and your environment can best match the frequency of your goal. Then we work on three things: the relationships your goal needs, the inner confidence to build and keep them, and the rest of your lifestyle.

Social connection is a pillar of health. It is one of the 6 pillars of lifestyle medicine, the framework behind the American College of Lifestyle Medicine and the American Board of Lifestyle Medicine. It is also the most overlooked: most check-ups ask about your diet, exercise and sleep, and almost never about who you spend your life with. Yet the U.S. Surgeon General compares the health risk of social disconnection to smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day. That is the pillar we specialize in, while we coach the other five alongside it.

The three things we work on

  • RelationshipsThe people your goal needs, and how you keep them in your life
  • Personal performanceAnxiety, confidence, fear and overthinking: out of your head, into the room
  • EnvironmentWhere you live, the places you go, and the systems that put you in front of the right people

The 6 pillars of lifestyle medicine we coach

  • NutritionWhat and how you eat
  • Physical activityHow you move
  • SleepHow you recover
  • StressHow you handle pressure
  • Social connection · our focusWho you spend your life with
  • Risky substancesAlcohol, nicotine and other habits

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is this for?

Ambitious professionals who want a different social life: a partner, real friends, mentors, a circle that matches where they're going, or the confidence to meet them.

Why an MD?

Your relationships, your stress and your surroundings are part of your health, and they shape every goal you have. Physicians are trained to find the one cause behind many symptoms. Karim earned his MD at McGill and completed his family medicine residency at McMaster, and uses that training to find what in your life is holding your goals back, then coaches you to change it.

Is it worth it?

Separate help for confidence, dating, networking and planning easily costs more, and none of it connects to your goals or your health. Here, one physician looks at your whole life, shows what to change, coaches you every week and gives you the systems. Covered by the guarantee.

I'm busy. How much time does it take?

Assessment: one 90-minute video session and 3 check-ups of 20 minutes. Core and Platinum add a 30-minute session every week for 30 days. Sessions run weekdays 12–3 pm and 6–9 pm, and Saturdays 10 am–12 pm.

What is the 7D assessment like?

A structured video conversation with Karim, not therapy. He asks about your goals, your vision and each dimension of your life. You choose what to share.

Is this dating coaching or networking coaching?

It covers both, and more. The goal is a healthier social life: the right people, the right places, and the confidence to keep them in your life.

Which bonus should I pick?

The one closest to your goal. Want new places to go? Your City Map. Want a full calendar without the searching? Your Event system. Want mentors and professional leads? Your LinkedIn system. Karim helps you choose after your assessment.

Can I get all 3 bonuses?

Yes, with Platinum ($1,997): everything in Core, plus your City Map, Event system and LinkedIn system.

Does the LinkedIn system message people for me?

No. It builds your list and drafts your messages. You read them and click send. Nothing is sent automatically.

What happens after 30 days?

You keep your report and your bonus. Retake your assessment: if your score hasn't improved and you did everything, Karim keeps coaching you free until it does, or you can choose a full refund.

*How does the guarantee work?

Retake your assessment at day 30. If your score hasn't improved and you did everything, we keep coaching you free until it does, or you can choose a full refund. Applies to Core and Platinum. "Did everything" means you attended every session and completed all homework and surveys. Free coaching or a full refund is your choice.

Why only 5 clients a month?

Every report is written by hand and every client gets weekly sessions with Karim. Five a month is how many he can do well.

Yes. Take the free assessment and see your map.

Is this healthcare or therapy?

No. It's MD-led coaching. Karim doesn't diagnose, treat or prescribe. When you need clinical care, you'll be referred to a licensed professional. Scope of service.
