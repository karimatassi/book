About

Karim Atassi, MD

Karim Atassi is a lifestyle doctor who helps high performers and executives reach their life goals by changing their environment instead of relying on willpower.

His focus is social health, which he calls the under-recognized pillar of longevity. He has helped hundreds of people find partners, find exits in their careers, reconnect with their purpose, change their habits and improve their finances.

It started in medical school. Karim kept seeing that most of his patients' problems came from their social health, yet the traditional model never asked about their careers, money, relationships, spiritual understanding or the information they feed their minds.
